Concepts
01 / 06 Letterform
The Notch B
A heavy architectural B with a sharp diagonal cut slicing through it — content that's already been edited down.
02 / 06 Silhouette
The Pinch
One fluid silhouette — wide at top, squeezed tight at the waist, releasing below. Organic compression made geometric.
03 / 06 Linear
Three to One
Three bold inputs converging into one decisive output. Many videos, one digest — the pipeline made visible.
04 / 06 Two-tone
The Fold
A page folded at one corner — the dog-ear of a document trimmed to its essence. Bold blue body, pale blue reveal.
05 / 06 Kinetic
Speed Mark
Two thick parallel slashes — the universal signal for fast-forward, bold enough to own a shirt or a favicon.
06 / 06 Geometric
The Lens
A bold C-shaped crescent — a circle with a focused opening. Pulls in everything and emits only what matters.
